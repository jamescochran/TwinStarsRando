Rules FAQ

Rulings from designer Jason Tagmire on BoardGameGeek and korossyl's community FAQ (general, characters, scenarios), which states every answer was approved by the designer. Each answer is labelled with who made the ruling and links to its source thread. Unofficial and paraphrased.

General rules

Can you place both character dice on the same character? Can one character take both skill dice?

No. Each character gets exactly one character die, and in a skill check each character also takes exactly one skill die. Only a special rule that says otherwise changes this.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3

Is each die tied to a character, or do you roll first and then choose where each die goes?

Roll first, then decide which character gets which die. The order you place them matters, because instants and effects resolve as each die is placed. Most of the game's decisions are about placement and what to re-roll.

Authority: Community Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

If two combos are possible at once, which one happens?

You choose which of the possible combos to activate. This comes up often in Hunt the Bounty and with characters that have two icons on one spot.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3 BoardGameGeek thread 4

When the symbols on the row form a combo, can you avoid it by choosing a symbol that wouldn't activate it?

No. If a combo is formed you must activate it, unless another combo is available that you can activate instead.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Can one roll trigger two combos if you have enough icons?

No, only one combo per roll. Resolving a combo returns both dice to your hand, so a second combo never gets the chance to fire.

Authority: Community Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Which abilities trigger when a die is reduced (knocked down)?
  • Instants and Effects trigger only when a die is placed after a roll (the first roll, an energy re-roll or a character's re-roll). A reduction never triggers them unless a card says so; the Series II rulebook states that they are not activated by the reduction.
  • Reactions and Fatigue trigger only when a die is reduced.
  • Icons or text with no Instant/Effect label apply whenever a die sits on that spot, however it got there. Examples: Kinglan's flip and Inpon Gol's broken heart.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3 BoardGameGeek thread 4

What is the sequence of events when activating a reaction or fatigue ability?

If a roll gives no combo, choose one die to reduce and one to re-roll. Reduce first. If the space you reduced to has a fatigue or reaction ability, resolve it. If that produces no combo, pick up the other die and re-roll it as usual. (Whether a combo can be checked straight after the reduction is disputed; see “When exactly do you check for a combo?”.)

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

When exactly do you check for a combo? Can a single reduced die complete one before the other die is re-rolled?

Normally you check once both dice are placed and their instants and effects have resolved. For a die you have just reduced, Jason Tagmire has given two different answers:

  • Rule the World (2017): reduce one die, then check for a combo (rare, but possible with one die), then move the Assault Track, then roll the other die.
  • Hunt the Bounty (2018): a die knocked down onto an alert “doesn't happen right away”, because combos are only checked once both dice are placed.

There is no later ruling. Pick one reading and use it consistently.

Authority: Disputed Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

If a combo doesn't occur, one die is reduced and the other re-rolled. If a combo still doesn't occur, must the same die be reduced again?

No. Each time, you choose which die to reduce and which to re-roll.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

Do you keep reducing and re-rolling until a combo happens? What if a die would go below 1?

Yes, repeat reduce-and-re-roll until a combo occurs. If a reduction would take a die below 1, you take the !! combo instead.

Authority: Rulebook Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Are instants optional? If an instant includes a skill check, when do you roll it, and does failing cancel the instant?

Instants are optional; effects are mandatory. You only roll an instant's skill check if you choose to use the instant. The instant happens either way and the check, with its bracketed penalty, comes afterwards. In Escape the Brig, the “any Instant” cost likewise applies only when you actually use one.

Authority: Community Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3

Can an instant trigger on a re-rolled die, or only on the first roll?

Any roll counts, including re-rolls. Reducing a die onto the spot does not.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

If you win by performing an action that requires a skill check, do you win immediately or perform the check first?

Do the skill check first and take its consequences. If the check knocks out a character, you lose instead. This includes reaching the escape space in Escape the Brig with a ✓✓ Advance.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3 BoardGameGeek thread 4

When gaining or losing health, can you choose which character is affected?
  • A heart icon on a character affects that character.
  • A heart icon on the scenario card affects either character, unless the card names one (some scenarios name the Primary or Secondary).
  • Health lost from a skill check can come from either character, unless the card names one (for example, the Doctor in Stop the Virus).

That is why Stag Solar's 1-spot heal can only heal Stag.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3

Do all special abilities cost energy? When can you use them?

No. The universal special (re-roll a die) costs energy, and many others do too, but a special costs only what it shows. Saaze's triggers when she would drop to 0 health, and Yanfred's is paid for by taking a !! effect. Specials can be used at any time, like the universal re-roll.

Authority: Designer-approved Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3

Does “dice” on a card mean character dice or skill dice?

Character dice, unless the card says otherwise.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Do the Series II rules replace the Series I rules?

Yes. The rules are the same, but the Series II rulebook is clearer and explains the two icons added in Series II.

Authority: Community Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

Which scenario should a new player start with?

Escape the Brig is the tutorial for the whole system; Master the Trials is the Series II tutorial. The pairings printed on scenario cards are not recommendations (see Components & errata).

Authority: Community Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

Is there an easier variant for when no combo occurs?

Players proposed reducing one die and keeping the other. Jason Tagmire suggested a softer version instead: reduce either die, then either keep the other or re-roll it. He warned that it makes the game much easier. It is a house rule, not errata.

Authority: House rule Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Dice & skill checks

When can you use the universal special ability to re-roll a die? What can it target?

Spend energy to re-roll any single die at any time: character dice, skill dice, or scenario dice such as experiment, capture and search dice. This holds on every difficulty. In Hunt the Bounty energy is one of the scenario's resources, so it can pay for a search-die re-roll too. Some characters change the cost: Bood may pay health instead of energy. Character re-roll abilities such as Bood's own instant affect character dice only.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3 BoardGameGeek thread 4

For a skill check, what happens if one die fits the range and the other doesn't?

You fail. Roll both skill dice and give one to each character; each die must fall in the range of the character it goes to. Jason Tagmire's example: Bood (4–6) and Stag Solar (3–4) roll 3 and 3. Stag can take one 3, but the other is outside Bood's range, so the check fails.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3

Do skill dice trigger the icons or instants on the number they show?

No. A skill check only asks whether each die fits a range; stars, alerts, checks and instant text on that number are ignored. Skill dice can still be re-rolled with the universal re-roll.

Authority: Community Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

A skill die fits my character's range, but my character die already sits on that number. Do I still pass?

No one has answered this on BGG. Character dice and skill dice are separate, and skill checks look only at ranges, so the occupied spot should not matter and you pass. Treat that as a reasonable reading, not a ruling.

Authority: Disputed Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

When a card says to re-roll both dice, are they rolled together or one at a time?

Together, as a fresh roll; then place them. Dain Taubo's 3-spot works this way, as do Bood's and Grant's re-rolls.

Authority: Community Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Does a re-roll add a second attempt, or replace the first?

It replaces it. For example, re-rolling an experiment in Confine the Quarks can't earn two quarks.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Scenarios

Scenario 01: Escape the Brig!

Do you need to acquire the Pick (with a ★★ combo) before you can use it?

Yes. The Pick starts covered; a ★★ combo uncovers it, and then you can use it at will.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

If you get a ★! combo without having acquired the Pick, must you take the “Advance forward 1 space and [Skill −2 Health]” action?

No. You can't use the Pick yet, so ★! is not a combo.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

If you acquire the Pick and later drop it, is it gone for good?

No. It goes back to its spot on the scenario card and can be taken again with another ★★ combo.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

“Any Instant: skill check or alert the guard.” Does this apply when you place a die on an instant but don't use it, or reduce onto one?

No. It applies only when you actually use an instant.

Authority: Community Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

Scenario 02: Confine the Quarks!

Do base precision and precision begin at the same spot?

Yes, both start on the first spot of the precision track.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3

Can precision ever be below base precision?

No. If precision is level with base precision when base rises, precision rises too.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2 BoardGameGeek thread 3

Do the bonuses on the precision track apply to any die roll, or only when experimenting?

Only when experimenting.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Are the precision-track bonuses cumulative?

Yes. At the top of the track you have all three.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

“May re-roll 1 if experimenting”: one die, or a die showing 1?

Every die showing 1 may be re-rolled when experimenting. If no die shows 1, this gives no re-roll.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

“May use 5 and 6 for +$1M”: does a five and a six each add funding, or only together?

Only one 5 together with one 6 adds $1M.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

When a 5 and 6 grants funding, does that experiment still count as a failure?

No. You gain the funding and that experiment ends as a near miss: neither a success nor a failure, and not a double.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

“Evens = +1 at Pentaquark”: which rolls add a quark? Does an even double give +2?

Doubles are normally required. Once you reach this point, two even numbers (for example 2 and 4) also give one quark. An even double (2/2, 4/4, 6/6) still gives just +1; Jason Tagmire said so “as far as I know” and planned to confirm with Mike Mullins, but no follow-up was posted.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

Does Calibrate raise base or current precision? Can you experiment twice in one turn?

Calibrate raises current precision by 1, then you experiment. You may also take the free experiment after any combo, which carries its downside, so a Calibrate turn can include two experiments.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

What does each combo do to the precision tracks?

★★ is Calibrate (+1 current). ✓✓ raises base by 1. !✓ is a skill check, with −1 current on a failure. !! drops current to base. Base never goes down.

Authority: Community Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

Scenario 03: Rule the World!

Are the enemy ships covered at the start of the scenario?

No; they start uncovered and ready to attack.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

How do the !! sneak attack and the Assault Track work?

On !!, roll two dice. A result matching an uncovered starfighter damages that fighter's target; even numbers and covered fighters do nothing. The Assault Track moves each time you reduce a die, not on !!.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

What is the order of events when you reduce a die?

Reduce one die and take the other in hand, check for a (single-die) combo, move the Assault Track and resolve its end, then roll the other die. A later ruling in another scenario conflicts with the combo check; see “When exactly do you check for a combo?” under General rules.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Who is Primary and who is Secondary?

You choose before the game. Put the Primary on your left. Stop the Virus uses the same convention.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

What does Ion Cannon damage do, and do you lose if it's hit while at Start?

It moves down one step. Players say a hit at Start does not lose the game, but this was never confirmed.

Authority: Disputed Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Scenario 04: Stop the Virus!

When using the ✓✓ combo to cover a virus, if you roll the number of the marked virus, can you cover the marked virus?

Yes, you got a lucky roll.

No; covering more viruses than the card lists gives no extra bonus.

No. Treating the marked virus has nothing to do with healing attempts, which heal or restore stamina and never touch the virus row.

At every attempt (a designer email relayed by a player).

For “Doctor [Skill −1 Stamina]”, does only the Doctor roll?

No. Both characters take the skill check as usual; the Doctor takes the penalty if it fails.

How many guards begin at the Guard Start location?

Just one; more may appear later.

In any direction.

Nothing; at most four guards are in play. An eliminated guard's token can be reused for a later guard.

If you move twice while carrying the plans and pass a spot that generates guards without stopping, do guards still appear?

Yes.

Does “If plans here” apply only at the marked spots?

No one answered. The plans start with one guard, and guards are added as the plans pass the marked points.

Scenario 06: Hunt the Bounty!

How many resources do you start with?

Both characters start at full health. You choose your starting energy (that sets the difficulty). You start with five supplies, which is also the maximum.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

What do the Inquiry and Restock actions do?

After placing both characters, roll both dice. If the total matches the condition on the planet where a character made an inquiry, gain the listed information; otherwise gain the listed threat. If either die fits the restocking character's skill range, gain one resource of your choice; if neither does, nothing happens.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Can you re-roll search (inquiry) dice? Can energy pay for it?

Yes. Each resource you spend re-rolls one die, and energy is one of this scenario's resources along with supplies and health. Jason Tagmire also confirmed that the basic energy ability can re-roll any die here, including capture and search dice.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

If an inquiry spans two planets, do both apply?

Yes. You can fail one and succeed at the other with the same roll.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Restock and Inquiry tokens: does either character's skill range count?

No. Each token belongs to one character (Primary or Secondary), and only that character's range counts.

Authority: Community Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

What does the “7” planet require?

The two dice must add up to exactly 7.

Authority: Community Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

How does Max Info or Max Threat “add a star/alert to combos” work?

Treat it as an imaginary third die sitting on one ★ (max info) or one ! (max threat). After instants and effects, check all three for a combo. One real ! plus the imaginary ! makes a !! ambush. You can manipulate the real dice to avoid it.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

How do you spend resources to attempt a capture on a ★★ combo?

Resources are supplies, health and energy. Spend as many as you like, then roll two dice. Your information level plus the dice plus the resources spent must reach 30 to capture.

Authority: Designer ruling Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

What if I'm not ready to capture on ★★?

Take the alternative instead: +1 information, +1 energy and +1 supply. A failed capture doesn't lose the game, but it drains you. Try not to reach 15 threat.

Authority: Community Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

Do you lose if you reach the end of the threat track?

No; you lose only when a character runs out of health. But a full threat track costs so much health on every !! combo that you won't last long.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Do you win if you reach the end of the information track?

No; you win only by capturing the bounty, though that is very unlikely without a nearly full information track.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Scenario 07: Master the Trials!

What does it mean that you take a skill check “at each reduction”?

Each time a die is reduced (because no combo happened), take a skill check; on a failure the character that reduced loses 1 health.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

How does the gap between the Master's and Apprentice's levels affect a trial?

To attempt a trial, roll one die; you succeed if it is at least the number on the next trial spot. When using the ✓✓ combo, subtract the gap between the Master's and Apprentice's levels from your roll (3 if both are still at their starting spots).

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Is Master the Trials supposed to be this easy?

Yes; it is the Series II tutorial. You win by completing three trials in order or by reaching the end of training. Pairs that combo on their own make it easier still.

Authority: Community Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Scenario 08: Beat the Odds!

The description says five coins but the scenario side says six. Which is it?

Five; the scenario side is a misprint. It is a best-of-five contest: the first to win three coins wins.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Once the thieves have moved a coin to their track, can you still recover it?

Yes. A ★★ combo steals a coin back while it is still on the thieves' track. Once it reaches the X, it is lost.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

What does it mean to “gain a re-roll” with the ★★ combo?

A ★★ combo can mark one of the four numbers on the re-roll track (instead of recovering a coin). The mark is permanent. From then on, when betting, if your total equals a marked number you may re-roll. Re-rolling a 6 or an 8 needs a skill check; failing costs 1 health but you still re-roll. The track only matters while betting.

Authority: Designer-approved Sources: BoardGameGeek thread 1 BoardGameGeek thread 2

How does betting with the ✓✓ combo work?

Bet on under 7, exactly 7, or over 7. If you hit, win a coin; if you won on 7, you must bet again. If you miss, lose 1 health.

Authority: Community Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Scenario 09: Control the Skies!

When beginning, do you place your character token or the enemy starfighter first?

Your characters' token first, then the enemy starfighter.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

When a new wave of enemies arrives, can you reposition your ship for free?

No. To move, you must activate a ✓✓ combo.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

If a ✓✓ combo moves you onto an enemy fighter, can you move without attacking?

No; if a ✓✓ combo lets you destroy a fighter, you must.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

How do you use the upgrade track?

Each ★★ combo moves the upgrade token forward. Reaching the missile gives you the missile token; reaching the shield gives you the shield token. At the end you may return to the start for free. Instead of advancing, a ★★ combo can deploy a missile or shield you already hold.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Could you gain a shield on every spot, or stockpile missiles?

No. There is one shield token and one missile token; once taken, each can't be gained again until it is used.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Scenario 10: Sell the Junk!

Do you begin with $0 or $1?

$1; place the token on the money track accordingly.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Does the money track really skip from $8 to $10 and from $10 to $12?

Yes. You need to gain $2 to make those jumps; a single dollar gained at $8 (or $10) is wasted.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

When the selling fee rises above the price, does anything reset? Do you lose health on every later rise?

Nothing resets until you sell, or until a ★★ barter drops the fee to $0. You lose 1 health only when the fee first passes the price.

Authority: Community Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Scenario 11: Destroy the Order!

After passing a law, must you always follow it, or can you choose when it applies?

A law in effect must be followed whenever it applies.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

What counts as “using” a law?

A law is used only when you activate a combo that the law changes in some way. An icon that doesn't produce a combo is not a use. (The source thread's “does result in a combo” is a typo for “does not”.)

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Scenario 12: Serve the Rabble!

Do the character tokens placed on “bouncer” and “server” ever move or interact with the scenario?

No; they only mark which character has which role.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

What is the difference between the server and the bouncer?

The bouncer starts with 1 health above the character's usual maximum. The server may, at any time, give up 1 health to gain 1 energy.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Scenario 13: Topple the Giant!

No rulings for Topple the Giant! have been published yet; its BoardGameGeek forum only has print-and-play availability threads. The general rules apply.

Scenario 14: Save the Spacewhales!

The !! combo says “roll to see if any spacewhales are lost”. What do you roll?

One plain die. If the junk is at least double the roll, you lose a spacewhale and both characters lose 1 health. It is not a skill check.

Authority: Community Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Droid Assistants

How are Droid Assistants used?

As an optional add-on: add one droid to your team before play. Droids have both helpful and harmful traits. There is no official droid rules thread on BGG.

Where can I get the droid cards and tokens?

In 2020 Jason Tagmire posted print-and-play links for the Series I and II droids; those links later broke, but the art is in the BGG image gallery and the files were reposted on the Button Shy Discord. The token art was a free download on PnP Arcade, and the deluxe print-and-play includes the droids.

SYZYGY campaigns

Components & errata

Should I follow the “Recommended With” pairings on scenario cards?

No. Jason Tagmire said they are purely thematic and “simply wrong”, and should be removed in a later printing. The characters were designed for the whole system, not for one scenario.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Saaze's printed card differs from early promo images. Which is right?

Play the printed card (! rather than ✓ on her 1-spot, and the extra skill text on her 3-instant differ). The promo images were early versions.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Was Scenario 02 renamed?

Yes, from “The Quark Star” to “Confine the Quarks!”.

Authority: Designer-approved Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

The Series III rulebook says effects may apply after a reduction. Is that right?

No, that wording is an error carried over from Series I and corrected in Series II: effects never trigger on a reduction.

Authority: Community Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

What size are the deluxe dice?

16 mm character dice and 12 mm skill dice.

Authority: Designer ruling Source (BoardGameGeek thread)

Tips & Features

New to Twin Stars?

Randomizing

  • Lock 🔒 any character or scenario to keep it when you randomize
  • Your last result and difficulty are remembered across sessions

During Play

  • Roll Dice appears below the timer once a mission is loaded
  • Add optional notes when you save a record, or later from the Mission Log
  • Tap a die to lock it (teal glow = locked) — locked dice keep their value on the next roll
  • Tap +/− on a locked die to fine-tune its value; wraps 6→1
  • Skill Check rolls white dice temporarily, then restores your normal dice exactly as you left them
  • Yanfred Jima — the ⇄ button swaps the two dice values (unlock both dice first)
  • Hebolt Rom — a Flip button appears under each die; it turns the die to the opposite face (7 − value)

Ambient Radio

  • Tap ♫ in the header to stream SomaFM Mission Control (online only)
  • Volume slider in the popover; lock-screen controls work too

Mission Log

  • Sort and filter records by date, result, difficulty, character, or scenario
  • Tap ✎ Edit on any record to change its result, difficulty or notes
  • Deleted something by mistake? Tap Undo in the message that appears
  • Export a backup of your records, or Import one, from the Mission Log tab

Stats

  • The Mission Log tab shows games played, win rate, how many combinations you've tried, and playtimes

Privacy

Your play data

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • Your Mission Log, notes, campaign and settings are stored only in this browser. They never leave your device unless you export a backup file yourself.

Anonymous usage counts

  • Page views and app events (for example “randomized”, or “record saved: Win, Normal”) are counted by a self-hosted Umami server run by the developer, to see which features get used.
  • No cookies and no personal identifiers. Character names, notes and your records are never sent. The parts of the address after ? or # are stripped.
  • If the app hits a JavaScript error, a short technical description (error text, file and line) is sent so it can be fixed.
  • If your browser sends Do Not Track, nothing is sent.

Other services

  • Fonts and all app files are served from this site. The only third parties the app contacts are the Umami server above (anonymous counts) and SomaFM, which streams the ambient radio and is contacted only when you press play.
  • Footer links (Ko-fi, BoardGameGeek, Discord and others) are only visited if you follow them.
